# DevFlow Features

Each capability is designed to serve a clear workflow while leaving room for progressive enhancement.

## 🔐 Authentication
**Purpose:** establish a secure, personalized entry point. **User story:** as a member, I can sign in and return to my workspace. **Future:** SSO, passkeys, session controls, and invited-member onboarding.

## 📊 Dashboard
**Purpose:** summarize what needs attention. **User story:** as a contributor, I can see priorities, progress, and recent activity at a glance. **Future:** configurable widgets and role-aware views.

## 🏢 Workspace and team management
**Purpose:** separate organizations and members. **User story:** as an admin, I can create a workspace and manage access. **Future:** roles, groups, audit logs, and enterprise provisioning.

## 📁 Projects
**Purpose:** give initiatives a durable home. **User story:** as a lead, I can define a project, its owner, status, and goals. **Future:** templates, dependencies, and project health scoring.

## ✅ Tasks
**Purpose:** capture actionable work. **User story:** as a developer, I can create, assign, prioritize, and complete work. **Future:** sub-tasks, estimates, blockers, recurring tasks, and rich attachments.

## 🧱 Kanban
**Purpose:** visualize work flow. **User story:** as a team, we can move tasks through meaningful statuses. **Future:** custom workflows, WIP limits, swimlanes, and board automation.

## 🗓️ Calendar and timeline
**Purpose:** expose time-based commitments and sequencing. **User story:** as a planner, I can see deadlines, sprints, and project milestones. **Future:** external calendar sync, dependency paths, and capacity overlays.

## 🔔 Notifications
**Purpose:** surface relevant change without unnecessary noise. **User story:** as a member, I receive actionable updates about my work. **Future:** granular preferences, digest delivery, and escalation policies.

## 🤖 AI assistant and sprint generator
**Purpose:** reduce planning overhead. **User story:** as a lead, I can turn a goal or backlog into a proposed sprint. **Future:** grounded context retrieval, approval workflows, and measurable suggestions. See [AI](AI.md).

## 🌊 Flow Mode and Focus Mode
**Purpose:** protect deep work. **User story:** as a contributor, I can reduce distractions and focus on the next best task. **Future:** ambient timers, break reminders, and personal productivity insights.

## 📈 Analytics
**Purpose:** make delivery trends legible. **User story:** as a manager, I can understand throughput and sprint health. **Future:** forecasting, anomaly detection, and team-custom metrics.

## 👤 Profile and settings
**Purpose:** let members tailor their experience. **User story:** as a user, I can adjust identity, preferences, and notification choices. **Future:** accessibility profiles and cross-workspace defaults.

## ⌘ Command palette and search
**Purpose:** make frequent actions immediate. **User story:** as a keyboard-oriented user, I can search and execute actions from anywhere. **Future:** natural-language commands, saved searches, and extension commands.

## 📱 Responsive layout and dark mode
**Purpose:** maintain a premium experience across devices. **User story:** as a user, I can work comfortably on any supported screen. **Future:** offline-friendly mobile companion and theme customization.

## 🔭 Future features

Integrations, developer tooling, automation, and multi-team portfolio planning are intentionally documented in [Future Ideas](FutureIdeas.md).
